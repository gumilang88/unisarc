About Unisarc

UNISARC is the premier automated Liquidity Provision Protocol built to power high-efficiency capital deployment and sustainable on-chain depth for the Arc ecosystem.

By integrating intelligent algorithmic vault routing with built-in Impermanent Loss Mitigation, UNISARC eliminates the core inefficiencies of decentralized market making. Our architecture continuously concentrates liquidity where volume occurs on Uniswap, maximizing real fee capture while actively shielding liquidity provider principal against market divergence.

Protected Liquidity Architecture

Advanced dynamic rebalancing designed to safeguard depositor principal from impermanent loss without sacrificing yield potential.

Volume-Driven Capital Efficiency

Automated liquidity routing that maximizes transaction fee accrual per unit of deposited capital.

Deflationary Value Flywheel

Protocol revenue and execution fees directly fuel the automated $UNISARC buyback-and-burn engine, permanently reducing supply as ecosystem volume expands.

Native Arc Infrastructure

Engineered from the ground up to serve as the foundational liquidity backbone starting Day 1 of Arc Public Mainnet.
